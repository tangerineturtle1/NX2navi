NX2navi and Triforce have merged and go on as Triforce.

- This update turns NX2navi's launcher into the door to Triforce: one window that starts Triforce Setup, Triforce's
  launcher.
- Nothing of NX2navi is removed or changed: your built game, your saves and your settings stay where they are.
- **Play NX2navi** in that window still starts the game you have, for as long as you want it.
- Triforce builds the game from your own files, as NX2navi did, and its Convert saves page brings your NX2navi saves
  over.

## Good to know

- **Already have NX2navi:** the launcher updates itself to this version at its next check, as for any update. There is
  nothing to download by hand.
- **New here:** get Triforce from https://triforceproject.com. This release carries no setup.
- **What is no longer in the launcher:** its own pages for settings, mods, rebuilding the game, saves and Add to
  Steam. The game's own settings menu is unchanged.
- **Mods:** Play NX2navi starts the game as it is in its folder. A mod added after this update is not built in.
- **Steam:** a shortcut made with Add to Steam opens this window; choose Play NX2navi there.
- **Convert saves in Triforce v0.3.6** asks for the folder with your saves: it is the one the window shows as
  *Your NX2navi game and saves* (Copy path).
- **Uninstalling NX2navi** from Windows' Apps list works as before and asks before it removes the game folder.
- **Linux:** the AppImage has no updater and is not changed by this release.

The window's source is in [`handover/`](https://github.com/tangerineturtle1/NX2navi/tree/main/handover).
