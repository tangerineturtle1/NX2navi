NX2navi is now Triforce
=======================

This version of NX2navi's launcher is the handover to Triforce: the two projects have merged and go on as Triforce.

launcher/start.pyw              what NX2navi.exe starts, as in every version
launcher/handover.py            the window: Get Triforce / Open Triforce, Play NX2navi, where your game files are
launcher/cli.py                 the command line's answer
launcher/selftest.py            pictures of the window for a look before release (NX2navi.exe --selftest --shots DIR)
launcher/art/                   the window's pictures (the Triforce crest; the fairy is NX2navi's logo)
launcher/triforce/              Triforce Setup, Triforce's launcher, as published at https://triforceproject.com,
                                with setup.json: its release, size and sha256. The window checks the file against
                                them before it starts it.

Triforce is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3. Its source code, Triforce Setup's
included, is at https://git.triforceproject.com/triforce/source

Nothing in this folder is game data, and nothing in it is derived from the game.
